Author Lifecycle

Every Author goes through a Well-defined Journey.

From enrollment to resignation, an author's status moves through a small, strict state machine - enforced on-chain at every step.

Registration and collateral - this is where every author's story begins.

1

Enroll

Author registers and locks collateral to join the network.

2

Probation

New authors start in probation - monitored before being trusted with permanence.

3

Eligible

Once probation elapses and no risk is outstanding, the author becomes eligible for permanence.

4

Elected

Selected by the network through the pluggable election process.

5

Active Author

Promoted to permanent status - full role responsibilities and network access.

6

Rewards / Penalties

Good behavior is rewarded. Misbehavior is penalized.

Enough risk sends an Active author back to Probation (step 2)
7

Resign

Once idle and free of pending penalties. Collateral is released; pending rewards are unaffected.

Probation exists because locking collateral alone doesn't prove reliability. Security starts before permanence.

Electing Authors

The Network Chooses its Authors.

When any duty pallet needs authors, it triggers an election. The election plugin - configured here - processes candidates and returns the selected authors.

Candidates line up their backing before I even get involved.

1

Candidates with backing

Supporters stake collateral behind the candidates they back.

2

Election Requested by duty pallet

Any duty pallet triggers an election when it needs authors.

3

Election Plugin processes election

Loads the configured model, runs election logic, and returns the selected authors.

4

Selected Authors

Selected authors become eligible for network duties.

5

Used by Duty Pallets

Other pallets use the elected authors for their responsibilities.

Elections are processed when required by any duty pallet. The election plugin is configured here.

Lots of Election Models - Fully Pluggable & Customizable

Flat (Aggregated)

All backing is aggregated per author into one influence weight.

Fair (Individual)

Every backing is its own weight - every contribution counts.

Quadratic

Reduces the influence of large backings to favor decentralization.

Capped

Applies an upper limit so no single backing can dominate.

Threshold

Only backings above a minimum threshold are counted.

Custom

Build and plug in your own election logic.

Runtime Governance

Governance
Steers the Network

Configure how block authors are managed by adjusting runtime parameters on-chain - without redeploying your runtime.

pallet-authors / lib.rs
GENESIS_CONFIG
#[pallet::genesis_config]
pub struct GenesisConfig<T: Config> {
    pub min_collateral: AuthorAsset<T>,
    pub max_exposure: AuthorAsset<T>,
    pub min_fund: AuthorAsset<T>,
    pub probation_period: BlockNumberFor<T>,
    pub reduce_probation_by: BlockNumberFor<T>,
    pub increase_probation_by: BlockNumberFor<T>,
    pub rewards_buffer: BlockNumberFor<T>,
    pub penalties_buffer: BlockNumberFor<T>,
    pub max_elected: u32,
    pub min_elected: u32,
    pub force_max_elected: bool,
}

Every field is a lever.

The eleven genesis fields group into three concerns: the economics of holding a role, the lifecycle an author moves through, and how elections resolve.

11
tunable parameters
0
redeploys to change them
root
origin to override
EconomicsLifecycleElections
min_collateral
Minimum collateral

The self-stake an author must lock to enrol. Cannot be zero.

default: 1
max_exposure
Maximum exposure

Caps how much a single backing operation may contribute.

default: max_value
min_fund
Minimum fund

Floor on any single backing contribution. Cannot be zero.

default: 1
probation_period
Probation period

Blocks a new or demoted author stays under review.

default: 10 blocks
reduce_probation_by
Probation relief

Blocks trimmed when good behavior is observed.

default: 1 block
increase_probation_by
Probation extension

Blocks added when unsafe behavior is detected.

default: 1 block
rewards_buffer
Rewards buffer

Delay before a reward finalizes, deterministic timing.

default: 3 blocks
penalties_buffer
Penalties buffer

Delay before a penalty enforces; room to remedy.

default: 4 blocks
max_elected
Maximum elected

Upper bound on authors chosen per election round.

default: 100
min_elected
Minimum elected

Quorum an election must reach to be valid.

default: 10
force_max_elected
Enforce the cap

True hard-truncates at max_elected; false lets all pass.

default: false
FLEXIBLE · ON-CHAIN · FUTURE-PROOF
Policy is data, not code.

Each field is overridable at runtime through ForceGenesisConfig, so the network evolves by changing values - never by shipping a new pallet.

ACCOUNTABLE BY DESIGN
You stay in control.

Overrides are gated to root or a governance origin. Parameters move; the guarantees around collateral, probation, and quorum don't.
